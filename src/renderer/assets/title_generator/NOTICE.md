# Minecraft Title Generator artwork

Font geometry and texture atlases are from
https://github.com/ewanhowell5195/MinecraftTitleGenerator, pinned in `manifest.json`.
They retain their original authorship and are not relicensed under MollyStudio's
code license. See `SOURCE-LICENSE.md` for the source repository's Minecraft asset notice.

- Minecraft Ten geometry and Flat: Ewan Howell.
- Minecraft Ten Cyrillic geometry and Flat: xllifi.
- Minecraft Five Bold geometry and Flat: Ewan Howell.
- Minecraft Dungeons geometry and Flat: ShrimpSnail and Ewan Howell.
- Minecraft Ten Version 2, Five Bold Block, Ten Outline, GUI, Seven and Five geometry and Flat: Ewan Howell.
- Minecraft Five Bold Cyrillic geometry and Flat: xllifi.
- Minecraft SGA geometry and Flat: Westbot.
- Block Serif geometry and Flat: DragonSlayr15001.
- Minecraft Ten Cracked: ShrimpSnail, Ewan Howell and MrBrose.
- Minecraft Ten Cyrillic Cracked: xllifi.
- Minecraft Ten Bevelled Gold: CL697 and ShrimpSnail.
- Minecraft Ten Cyrillic Bevelled Gold: CL697, ShrimpSnail and jackeyrolz.
- Minecraft Five Bold Bevelled Gold: PopitTart.

MollyStudio's mesh assembly, WebGL renderer, editor and project integration are
separate implementations. The Blockbench plugin is not executed or bundled.

All 309 registered texture presets/variants, 58 overlays, 148 tileables, the
original preview images and three intrinsic font overlays are bundled locally.
Each entry's original author is retained in `material-catalog.json`,
`manifest.json` and the generated renderer catalog. `manifest.assetPaths` maps
every bundled item to its source path, and `sourceHashes` records SHA-256 hashes
for the 913 pinned source files. Tileables in `tileables/minecraft` and the
specific font textures named in `SOURCE-LICENSE.md` retain Mojang's notice and
Minecraft EULA terms. They are not relicensed as MollyStudio code.

Original model mode uses the source cuboids and UVs. Optional closed contour
profiles are derived locally from the original front artwork and UVs by
`scripts/vendor-title-assets.py`; they do not change the artwork's attribution.
The three opaque fallback colors in `contour-swatches.png` are generated by
MollyStudio for fonts whose source atlas has no extrusion or border swatches.

## Cyrillic support

Only the original Minecraft Ten Cyrillic and Minecraft Five Bold Cyrillic models
and their matching artwork are used for Russian. Minecraft Ten Cyrillic also
contains the full Ukrainian alphabet. Credits remain as listed above and in the
manifest. MollyStudio filters native assets by each row's language; it does not
redistribute generated English-to-Cyrillic UV remaps or substitute letter shapes.
